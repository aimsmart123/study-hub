📖 Chapter Notes, Concepts, Formulas, Tips & Tricks (Arithmetic Progressions) ▼
1. Definition of Arithmetic Progression & Common Difference Foundations
An Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) is a list of numbers in which each term is obtained by adding a fixed number $d$ to the preceding term, except the first term $a$.
General Form: $a, a + d, a + 2d, a + 3d, \dots, a + (n-1)d$
• First Term: Denoted by $a$ or $a_1$.
• Common Difference: $d = a_{k+1} - a_k$ for any integer $k \ge 1$.
• Crucial Property: The common difference $d$ can be positive, negative, or zero.
• Finite vs Infinite AP: An AP containing a finite number of terms has a distinct last term $l$; an infinite AP continues indefinitely without a last term.
2. General Term ($n^{ ext{th}}$ Term) of an AP Core Formula
The $n^{ ext{th}}$ term $a_n$ (also called the general term) of an AP with first term $a$ and common difference $d$ is given by:
$$a_n = a + (n - 1)d$$
• If there are $m$ terms in an AP, then $a_m$ represents the last term, often denoted by $l$: $$l = a + (m - 1)d$$
• Linear Nature: The $n^{ ext{th}}$ term of an AP is always a linear polynomial in $n$: $$a_n = An + B$$ Here, the coefficient of $n$ is always the common difference: $d = A$.
• Checking a Term: To check if a number $X$ is a term of an AP, equate $a + (n-1)d = X$ and solve for $n$. $X$ is a term if and only if $n$ is a positive natural integer ($n \in \{1, 2, 3, \dots\}$).
3. $n^{ ext{th}}$ Term from the End of an AP End Term
Let an AP have first term $a$, common difference $d$, and last term $l$:
$$n^{ ext{th}} ext{ term from the end} = l - (n - 1)d$$
• Alternative Method (Reversing the AP): Reverse the sequence so that the last term becomes the first term ($a' = l$), and the common difference becomes negative of the original ($d' = -d$). Then compute $a'_n = l + (n-1)(-d)$.
• From Beginning vs End: In an AP with $m$ total terms, the $n^{ ext{th}}$ term from the end is the $(m - n + 1)^{ ext{th}}$ term from the beginning.
4. Three, Four & Five Terms in AP Algebraic Selection
When the sum of terms in AP is given, symmetric choice of terms simplifies algebraic computation drastically:
• Three numbers in AP: Let them be $$a - d, \quad a, \quad a + d$$ Sum $= (a-d) + a + (a+d) = 3a$. (Directly yields middle term $a$!).
• Four numbers in AP: Let them be $$a - 3d, \quad a - d, \quad a + d, \quad a + 3d$$ Sum $= 4a$. (Note: Here common difference is $2d$!).
• Five numbers in AP: $$a - 2d, \quad a - d, \quad a, \quad a + d, \quad a + 2d$$ Sum $= 5a$ (common difference $d$).
• Condition for 3 Numbers in AP: If $a, b, c$ are in AP, then: $$b - a = c - b \implies 2b = a + c \iff b = rac{a + c}{2}$$ Here $b$ is the Arithmetic Mean (AM) of $a$ and $c$.
5. Sum of First $n$ Terms ($S_n$) Sum Formulas
The sum $S_n$ of the first $n$ terms of an AP with first term $a$ and common difference $d$ is:
$$S_n = rac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$$
• When First and Last Terms are Known: $$S_n = rac{n}{2}[a + l]$$
• Sum of First $n$ Positive Natural Numbers: $$S_n = 1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + n = rac{n(n + 1)}{2}$$
• Sum of First $n$ Odd Positive Integers: $$S_n = 1 + 3 + 5 + \dots + (2n - 1) = n^2$$
• Sum of First $n$ Even Positive Integers: $$S_n = 2 + 4 + 6 + \dots + 2n = n(n + 1)$$
6. Fundamental Relations, Traps & Double Answers HOTS & Traps
• Relation Between $a_n$ and $S_n$: $$a_n = S_n - S_{n-1} \quad (n \ge 2), \qquad a_1 = S_1$$
• Quadratic Form of $S_n$: $S_n$ is always a quadratic in $n$ with zero constant term: $S_n = An^2 + Bn$. The common difference is always double the coefficient of $n^2$: $$d = 2A, \quad a = A + B$$
• Double Answers in $S_n$: When $d < 0$, solving $S_n = k$ produces two positive integer values of $n$ (e.g. $n = 4$ and $n = 13$ for $S_n = 78$ in $24, 21, 18\dots$). Physical Meaning: The terms from the 5th to the 13th term sum to zero because positive and negative terms cancel each other.
• Ratio of Sums to Ratio of Terms: Given $ rac{S_m}{S_n}$, to find the ratio of $m^{ ext{th}}$ to $n^{ ext{th}}$ terms $ rac{a_m}{a_n}$, substitute $m o 2m-1$ and $n o 2n-1$.
